Mock Tapeout

Learn chip tapeout on real boards, in a week, for tens of euros.

A first chip tapeout is slow and expensive, and once the silicon is back nothing inside it can be seen. Mock Tapeout is an open virtual fab: your RTL goes through the same steps as a chip (synthesis, placement, routing, DRC, LVS, timing signoff), but the "die" is a printed circuit board and the standard cells are ordinary logic ICs.

Boards come back in about a week, a failed design costs tens of euros to redo, not another shuttle, and every internal net can be probed with a logic analyser.

Who it is for

  • Students and teachers of digital and VLSI design who want a hands-on tapeout lab.
  • Anyone preparing a first silicon design for an open shuttle such as Tiny Tapeout, who wants to practise the flow first.
  • Makers who build logic-chip computers and want a real design flow behind them.

Status

Mock Tapeout is in early development. The PDK, the flow and the course material will be released under free licences. If you teach a course and would like to run a pilot shuttle, or just want to hear when the first shuttle opens, write to serge@rabyking.com.
